Welcome to Protech Building Products

We specialize in cosmetic and protective building products

Introduction

Protech specialises in the procurement of quality wooden flooring, decking, waterproofing, coatings & specialised building products. Apart from locally made speciality products, we source material from the EU, South America, the Far East, Indian Ocean Islands & other parts of Africa.

All materials supplied by us are offered with full technical support and accreditation by international and local authorities. We work closely with specialist installers and applicators to ensure that our products perform to optimum manufacturers’ specifications.

Our products meet or exceed industry quality requirements and provide excellent value for money. We provide technical support for all our products and are involved in applicator training and education.
